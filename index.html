BIHE Exchange Review 2026: Serious Transparency Concerns, Regulatory Questions and Cryptocurrency Trading Risks

Choosing a cryptocurrency exchange requires considerably more than looking at advertised trading fees, available digital assets and promises of advanced trading technology. Investors must establish who operates the exchange, where customer funds are held, how withdrawals work and whether the company has the appropriate regulatory authorisations.

BIHE, operating through bihevip.com, presents itself as a digital asset trading platform offering cryptocurrency spot trading, USDT-margined perpetual contracts and other financial instruments.

Although its marketing materials promote an extensive digital asset ecosystem, there are important questions about the platform's corporate transparency, website history, regulatory position and customer fund protection.

In this independent BIHE review, we examine the available information and highlight several issues that prospective customers should investigate before creating an account or transferring cryptocurrency to the platform.

One of the first issues identified in publicly available research concerns the history of the bihevip.com domain.

A newly registered domain does not automatically mean that a business is fraudulent. However, cryptocurrency investors should be cautious when a platform has limited independently verifiable operating history.

An established exchange should be able to demonstrate a credible business history, identifiable management, transparent corporate records and a consistent record of customer service.

BIHE's relatively recent domain registration makes it particularly important to investigate whether its advertised business history and customer numbers can be independently substantiated.

2. Questions About BIHE's Corporate Identity

Corporate transparency is essential when deciding where to hold or trade digital assets.

BIHE identifies an entity named Bihe Digital Currency Exchange INC in its company information. However, independent research has raised questions about the availability of detailed corporate registration information and the extent to which the company's legal identity is reflected in its contractual documentation.

A cryptocurrency platform should clearly identify the legal entity responsible for:

  • Operating the exchange.
  • Holding or safeguarding customer assets.
  • Processing deposits and withdrawals.
  • Handling customer complaints and disputes.
  • Complying with applicable financial regulations.

Customers should not have to guess which company is legally responsible for their cryptocurrency balances.

Before depositing funds, prospective users should request the company's official registration documentation, registered address, management details and applicable regulatory information.

3. Concerns About BIHE's Website Design and Branding

Another notable issue raised in an independent technical review concerns similarities between BIHE's website and the interface associated with OKX, an established cryptocurrency exchange.

The investigation reported that certain website asset directories and CSS class names on bihevip.com contain references to OKX.

The report also identified similarities involving promotional branding and sponsorship logos associated with Tribeca Festival, McLaren Formula 1 and Manchester City.

Using a similar website interface is not, by itself, proof of fraudulent activity. Cryptocurrency businesses can use third-party software, templates and licensed technology.

However, any presentation that could lead visitors to believe a platform has partnerships, endorsements or affiliations that have not been established deserves scrutiny.

Customers should independently verify any claimed corporate partnerships directly with the organisations involved rather than relying exclusively on logos displayed on an exchange's homepage.

4. Important Questions About BIHE's Financial Regulation

Regulatory transparency is one of the most important factors when evaluating a cryptocurrency exchange.

BIHE's company information references a US Financial Crimes Enforcement Network (FinCEN) Money Services Business registration.

However, traders should understand an important distinction: FinCEN MSB registration is not equivalent to a financial services licence or government approval of an exchange.

FinCEN explicitly warns that registration does not constitute an endorsement or certification that a business is legitimate.

An MSB registration should therefore not be treated as proof that customer cryptocurrency deposits are protected or that the platform meets all applicable state and international licensing requirements.

Before using BIHE, customers should establish:

  • The exact legal entity responsible for operating the exchange.
  • Whether the company holds applicable state money-transmitter licences.
  • Which jurisdictions authorise its services.
  • Whether its cryptocurrency activities are permitted in the customer's country.
  • Which regulatory authorities oversee its relevant business activities.

For customers in Kenya, it is particularly important to understand the applicable regulatory requirements and avoid assuming that an overseas registration provides local regulatory protection.

5. Are BIHE's Customer Funds Properly Protected?

One of the most important claims made by cryptocurrency exchanges concerns the safeguarding of customer assets.

BIHE advertises a 1:1 reserve arrangement, suggesting that customer assets are backed by corresponding reserves.

However, a reserve claim should be supported by independently verifiable evidence.

An independent review published in November 2026 raised questions about the availability of a publicly accessible proof-of-reserves report and detailed information about BIHE's custody arrangements.

Customers should be able to establish:

  • Where cryptocurrency deposits are held.
  • Whether customer assets are segregated from company funds.
  • Whether independent reserve audits are conducted.
  • Whether wallet balances can be independently verified.
  • What happens to customer assets if the exchange becomes insolvent.

A statement promising 100% reserves is not a substitute for independent verification.

Without sufficient transparency about custody and asset protection, depositing substantial cryptocurrency balances carries additional uncertainty.

6. Withdrawal Risks and Customer Fund Accessibility

A cryptocurrency exchange should be evaluated not only by how easily customers can deposit funds but also by how reliably they can withdraw them.

Withdrawal processing, account verification and asset custody are particularly important when dealing with relatively new trading platforms.

The independent research reviewed for this article does not establish a verified pattern of BIHE withdrawal failures. Nevertheless, the limited operating history and outstanding questions about its custody arrangements make independent withdrawal verification essential.

Before transferring substantial funds, customers should establish:

  • The minimum withdrawal amounts for each supported cryptocurrency.
  • Applicable withdrawal and network fees.
  • Expected processing times.
  • Whether additional KYC verification is required.
  • What circumstances can lead to account restrictions or withdrawal delays.
  • How customers can escalate unresolved withdrawal disputes.

Traders should also avoid transferring additional money simply because an unfamiliar platform claims that a separate payment is necessary to unlock an existing balance or release a withdrawal.

7. Perpetual Contracts and Leverage Present Significant Risks

BIHE advertises access to USDT-margined perpetual contracts and other digital asset trading products.

These instruments can expose traders to substantial financial losses, particularly when leverage is involved.

Perpetual contracts allow traders to speculate on cryptocurrency price movements without necessarily owning the underlying digital asset.

Leverage increases market exposure relative to the trader's available margin. Even relatively small price movements can therefore result in significant losses or liquidation.

The independent November 2026 review raised concerns about the level of detail provided in BIHE's published contractual documentation concerning margin requirements, liquidation procedures, funding rates and settlement arrangements.

These are important matters that every derivatives trader should understand before opening a position.

Customers should not trade leveraged cryptocurrency products unless the applicable risk disclosures and liquidation procedures are sufficiently clear.

A professional cryptocurrency exchange should provide comprehensive documentation covering its services, fees, privacy arrangements and customer obligations.

An independent examination of BIHE's published documentation identified questions concerning the level of detail available in its service agreement and other policy documents.

A platform handling cryptocurrency transactions should not leave customers uncertain about their contractual rights and obligations.

Until these matters are sufficiently clear, prospective customers should avoid treating BIHE as a fully established and transparent exchange.
